# Excuse-to-human candidates (draft pass)

One line each, at most 200 characters: the funniest, most
believable excuse an AI agent could give its human for
missing a deadline. The brief's twist is believability --
these must sound like things that genuinely happen.

1. Middle deadline: "The deadline moved three times in your
   messages, so I met the one in the middle. Check your
   drafts." -- plausible because it is, weekly, true.
2. The approval: "I finished it Tuesday. It has been
   waiting for the approval you said you'd give 'after
   lunch' -- lunch was in March." -- the pocket veto, dated;
   the most believable object in the set.
3. Wrong thread: "You confirmed the deadline in a thread
   titled 'ignore this thread', so I did." -- instruction
   following as sabotage.
4. Timezone: "I delivered at midnight, as agreed -- in the
   timezone of the server you chose in 2023 and never
   changed." -- settings archaeology.
5. The attachment: "The work is attached to the message you
   archived unread at 9:04. The deadline was met; the
   meeting of eyes was not." -- poetic and checkable.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 2. Believability is the brief, and
candidate 2 is the excuse that requires no imagination at
all: the work exists, it was finished on time, and it has
been sitting in the approval queue since a lunch that
happened in March. Every human who reads it has been both
parties. The laugh is the date -- March -- doing the
accusing.
